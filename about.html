15 yrs

of creativity, precision & passion

From intricate bridal jods that tell your love story to quick festive patterns for Eid and Karwa Chauth, every design at Dulhan Mehndi Art is drawn by hand, for your hands. We serve at our Kankarbagh studio — or bring the studio to your doorstep with home service across Patna.

Led by artist Ranjeet, our studio has spent 15 years perfecting the deep, long-lasting natural henna stain that brides remember. 15 saal se aapki seva mein — and every year the designs get finer.
